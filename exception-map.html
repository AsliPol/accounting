The four exceptions

Most expensive

Missing entry

We invoiced it. They never posted it. Nothing on our side looks wrong, so it hides until someone compares.

Cost: revenue never collected · Fix: partner posts it
Upstream cause

Amount mismatch

Both systems have the invoice, for different figures. The gap is usually pricing or currency, set before either ledger saw it.

Cost: the difference · Fix: pricing or FX correction
Silent

Duplicate posting

The same entry recorded twice on their side. A plain lookup will not find it — it stops at the first match and reports everything as fine.

Cost: overstated balance, payment dispute later · Fix: reversal
Unknown until traced

Unmatched transaction

On their statement, absent from ours. Either something we failed to record, or their error. Until it is traced, we do not know which.

Cost: unquantified · Fix: depends entirely on cause
Everything else is a match — typically 90%+ of rows. The whole point of building the process well is never having to look at them.
