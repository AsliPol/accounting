About

I grew up between two worlds most people would not put together: greenhouses and tourism. My family's living came from both, in the Mesopotamian region of Turkey, and the dinner table conversation was always the same shape — what did it cost to bring in, what did we actually get back, and where did the difference go.

I took that question into finance. For three years I ran accounts receivable at a EUR 500M company, reporting directly to the CFO, finding money that had quietly gone missing: the transaction that never posted, the entry that posted twice, the amount coded to the wrong place.

Then I moved to Canada and made a deliberate choice. Rather than assume my experience would transfer, I earned a Canadian credential — a full-time Ontario College Diploma at Fanshawe College with a 3.90 GPA and Dean's Honour Roll standing, and my QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor certification alongside it.
